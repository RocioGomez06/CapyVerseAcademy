A classroom tool,
not just a game

CapyVerse Academy was created at the Instituto Federal Sul Rio-Grandense as part of the Técnico em Análise e Desenvolvimento de Sistemas program, originally for the Inglês Técnico I course.

The idea is simple: programming concepts click faster when you can see and interact with them. No abstract lectures — just Capy, a problem, and a way to solve it.

Every game runs in any browser with no install needed, and the Bang Wong palette ensures the interface works for everyone in the room.
